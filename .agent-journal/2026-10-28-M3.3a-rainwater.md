# 2026-10-28 — M3.3a: rainwater tank

Roles: researcher (research/15), then system designer (12i, 12c-11). Input: drawing 3.3.2.5 v2, DS1603DA-V2.0 datasheet.

## Decisions (Tomas)

- D1 The rainwater tank is a driver of its own (12i, `driver-rainwater`, kinds `rainwater.*`): all logic there, raw endpoints, shown and controlled in the Inspector. Not a user-facing device.
- D2 Home side (12c-11; 12c-10 is Thermostat): `home.RainwaterTank` in `driver-home` (overview, mode auto/off, refill/fillUpper/stop/ack); templates shipped by `driver-rainwater`; a `WaterValve` from the fixed `supply` endpoint is the irrigation master (`fault.blocked`, irrigation unchanged); `WaterLeakDetector` for `retentionHigh` only; `refillFailed` on the tank only.
- D3 Static facts in config YAML (tank sizes, endpoints); dynamic settings in KV, editable from the Inspector (cutoff/resume included).
- D4 `supply` is a fixed endpoint, not configurable.
- D5 Descaler bypass on another PLC, raw output, reached by a WS client — a general mechanism (05a), not 12i-local. Bypass on → lead delay → mains; mains off → lag delay (KV) → bypass off. No bypass ⇒ no mains refill, no exception.
- D6 Pump always powered except at cutoff; filling the upper tank needs the pump and stops at cutoff.
- D7 Forecast-driven early transfer: Open → M3.4.
- D8 DS1603DA-3R on its own RS-485 port; definition `modbus/dyp/ds1603da-3r`, kind in 07a (third-party kinds), mention in 09 §7.
- D9 Tanks are boxes (linear), heights ≤ 2000 mm.
- Defaults accepted silently: WL.009.01 = upper sensor; pump in the accumulation tank; refill/transfer amounts relative; mains takes over when upper runs empty; floats as fallback; mains lockout until `ack`; retention high = alarm only.

## My choices inside the decisions (to confirm with Tomas)

- Processes are level-triggered (start whenever the condition holds and nothing runs); `stop` blocks a restart until the condition was false once.
- `lowPct`/`highPct` settings stand in for missing or unreachable floats.
- Upper level during a drain is an estimate (last value at rest − what the lower tank gained), checked at rest at `upperEmptyPct`.
- `off` mode powers the pump off (garden taps too).
- `RainwaterTank.mode` settable `auto`/`off` only; `manual` is the Inspector's.
- `control.parts` lists the driver's tails so the bundle never parses a tail.
- KV unreachable at start ⇒ defaults, `auto`.
