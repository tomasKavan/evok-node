Role: system designer. Input: research/matter/09.

## Decisions (Tomas)

- D1 Matter-shaped valve: `open({seconds?, level?})`, `close`, `state {current?, target}`.
- D2 every valve closes after a restart; no `startUp`.
- D3 `maxOpenS` on every opening → close + `fault.timeout`.
- D4 thermal valve takes `level` (duty cycle in the template); Thermostat sets it later.
- D5 valve exercise in the thermal template (7 d / 5 min).
- D6 two templates: `valve-from-output`, `thermal-valve-from-output`.
- D7 always Water Valve on Matter, no plug-in-unit projection (Apple shows nothing, accepted).
- D8 `IrrigationSystem` is a type. Reason "not a type" came from the wrong research claim (no Matter type) and from rules that cannot wait. No schedules, no rain logic; run time = zone `defaultOpenS` × multiplier; nothing saved.
- D8f pump role: `unipi.ro` now; later the tank device, which only gets a water-supply demand. Open, no task.
- D8g master: `unipi.ro` | `home.WaterValve`; never a Matter child.
- D9 roles may take home DeviceTypes; no cycle check (admin's responsibility).
- D10 a zone opened elsewhere closes the active zone.
- D11 16a: third product link — a device that owns the parent endpoint.
- D12 no leak latch. D13 matter-origin BSC: sensorFault, alarm, suppressAlarm; no sensitivity. D14 Rain/Freeze deferred. D15 no new rule kinds.
- File number 12c-08 (07 taken by Closures).

## My choices inside the decisions (to confirm with Tomas)

- `run` while running appends; after a zone opened elsewhere closes, the queue continues from the next zone.
- `maxOpenS` counts from the opening, not reset by a repeated `open`.
- Zones max 30 (Common Number tags end at 30).
- Matter `Start` → `run({})`; `OperationCompletion` when the queue runs out.
- `leak-from-di` `delayMs` applies to both edges.
